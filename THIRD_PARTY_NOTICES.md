# Third-Party Notices

No third-party source code, firmware, schematics, captures, or binaries are
vendored at this stage.

Candidate dependencies and prior art are documented in
[docs/research/oss-landscape.md](docs/research/oss-landscape.md). Inclusion in
that research file is not incorporation into this project. Before vendoring or
linking a dependency, record its exact version, source URL, license, copyright
notice, modification status, and distribution obligations here.

Do not commit Google Mobile Services, Widevine keys/blobs, Apple MFi
authentication material, vehicle secrets, or competitor firmware.
