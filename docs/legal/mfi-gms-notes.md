# CarPlay, Android, DRM, and Certification Notes

An open Android build does not automatically include Google Mobile Services,
Play certification, or Widevine L1. Do not promise commercial streaming-app
compatibility based on AOSP alone, and never redistribute proprietary Google or
DRM material.

Apple's Made for iPhone/iPad program governs licensed CarPlay accessories and
authentication. Open-source projection software does not supply permission,
certification, or lawful authentication credentials. Never publish, clone, or
solicit MFi keys or authentication devices.

For early prototypes, use open local applications, standards-compliant media,
and external devices connected through documented HDMI/DisplayPort and USB HID
interfaces. Treat branded projection systems as separate, optional integration
projects with their own legal and certification review.
