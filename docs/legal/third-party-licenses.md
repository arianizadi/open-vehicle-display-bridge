# Third-Party Licensing Procedure

Before a dependency enters a build:

1. pin an immutable version and source;
2. identify every applicable license and notice;
3. document static/dynamic/process boundaries;
4. check source-offer, installation-information, attribution, patent, and
   network-use obligations;
5. generate an SBOM;
6. preserve the upstream license and modifications; and
7. review compatibility with this repository's license for that path.

Potential GPL projects such as projection stacks must not be copied into a
permissively represented component. A separate-process interface can improve
modularity but does not erase license obligations. Hardware references are
prior art, not drop-in designs, unless their exact license and electrical fit
are verified.
